Has a probation practitioner told you to add conditions to this licence?

A probation practitioner such as a community probation practitioner or a probation POM must have told you which conditions to add. If not, you can still create a licence, but without being able to add any conditions.
Before you can add conditions, you must enter the details of the practitioner who told you to do this.
You can generate a licence that contains standard conditions and an additional condition telling this person not to approach or communicate with any victims of their offences.
