Licence submitted for approval

What happens next

The licence has been submitted for approval. This is usually done by the prison governor.

Once the licence has been approved, it can be printed from this service.

If you need to make changes

You can edit the licence until the person is released.

Changes to additional or bespoke licence conditions must be approved again. You must enter the details of the probation practitioner who requested the changes.

You can change community probation team contact details and initial appointment details without getting approval again.

After the person is released, only the community probation team can make changes to the licence.
