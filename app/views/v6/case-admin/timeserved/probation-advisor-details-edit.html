Who told you which conditions to add or change? 

Before you can add or change conditions, you must enter the details of the probation practitioner who requested this. For example, this could be a community probation practitioner or probation POM.

Confirm you will keep evidence of this request

You must keep evidence that this probation practitioner told you which conditions to add or change. For example, emails. This evidence may be needed if there is a serious further offence review.
