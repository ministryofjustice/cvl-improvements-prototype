Pentonville (HMP) will create this licence

Pentonville (HMP) will create a licence for this person because they are being released immediately following sentencing having served time on remand.

Once this person is released, you must review this licence and then record that you have done so in this service. After release, you will also be able to vary the licence to make any changes that are needed.

Initial appointment

If you need Pentonville (HMP) to set the initial appointment for a specific date and time or if no appointment is required, you must contact them to request this. You can email test@test.gov.uk

Otherwise, the prison can schedule the appointment for immediately after release or by 2pm the next working day.

When no initial appointment is needed
Select no appointment needed if this person is being released in the final third of a standard determinate sentence and is not:
  • serving a sentence for rape, serious sexual abuse against children or organised child sexual exploitation or grooming offences
  • MAPPA eligible
  • named on a child protection plan
  • serving a sentence for a state threat or a terrorist risk offence
  • tier A, B or C
  • serving a standard determinate sentence plus

Licence conditions

For time-served releases only, Pentonville (HMP) can add conditions to this licence if they are told to do so by a probation practitioner. If not, they can create a licence containing standard conditions and the following additional condition.

Making or maintaining contact with a person
Not to approach or communicate with any victims of your offences without the prior approval of your supervising officer.

You can email test@test.gov.uk to ask them to add specific conditions or you can add conditions after release.
